# Avitus Materia OS — Product Vision

## Vision
Avitus Materia OS is the digital operating system of Avitus Materia: a unified environment that acquires customers, helps them design and configure products, converts demand into accurate commercial offers, coordinates execution and learns from every completed project.

The long-term ambition is broader than one workshop. The architecture should support evolution from an internal operating system into a distributed manufacturing and collaboration platform for woodworking companies, specialist subcontractors, designers, installers and suppliers.

## What the system should become
### 1. Acquisition Engine
Continuously connect internet demand to measurable business outcomes. Attribute leads and revenue to channels such as Avitus-Materia.com, TikTok, Google, Instagram, Facebook, Pinterest, YouTube, referrals, architects and future channels.

### 2. Sales OS
Turn a lead into a structured opportunity, configuration, price calculation, quote, negotiation and order while preserving full history and economics.

### 3. Customer Experience Platform
Give customers a premium digital experience: account, product creator, AI advisor, saved ideas/configurations, visualization in their own interior, quotes, approvals, payments, progress, media, delivery and after-sales.

### 4. Data & Knowledge Core
Convert historical projects, prices, lead sources, work times, costs, materials, quality issues and outcomes into a long-term company asset.

### 5. Operations OS
Coordinate orders, projects, tasks, materials, purchasing, production, quality, logistics, installation and financial performance.

### 6. AI & Automation Layer
Start with analysis and recommendation, then progressively automate low-risk work. AI should eventually reason across the business but act only through controlled tools and policies.

### 7. Expansion Platform
Prepare for partner organizations, distributed manufacturing, SaaS and marketplace/network capabilities without burdening the initial MVP with enterprise complexity.

## Product principles
- Customer value and business value before technical fashion.
- One source of truth per business fact.
- Historical traceability rather than destructive overwrites.
- Configuration, quote and production data must remain connected.
- Global-first foundations: currencies, languages, tax/localization boundaries and organization scoping must not require a rewrite later.
- Mobile and shop-floor usability matter alongside desktop administration.
- AI is a force multiplier, not a substitute for data integrity.

## Product constitution
A feature earns priority when it materially improves at least one of:
1. revenue
2. margin
3. conversion
4. customer experience
5. operational efficiency
6. quality of decisions
7. scalability

## Strategic loops
The system should create closed learning loops. Example:

`content -> lead -> quote -> order -> actual cost/time -> margin -> satisfaction -> referral/repeat -> better attribution/pricing/AI`

A marketing channel is valuable not because it creates views or even leads, but because it creates profitable customers and long-term relationships.

## Five-year direction
The desired maturity path is:

1. people record structured data
2. system understands current business state
3. system detects risks and opportunities
4. system recommends actions
5. system executes approved actions
6. system autonomously performs bounded low/medium-risk operations
7. system orchestrates work across a partner/manufacturing network

Human leadership retains strategy, relationships, creativity and final responsibility.
