# Avitus Materia — Brand & Visual Direction

**Status:** approved working direction
**Date:** 2026-09-26

## Core feeling
Avitus Materia should look premium, restrained, material-led and timeless rather than trendy or technology-first.

The visual references approved in project work combine:
- warm ivory / parchment backgrounds,
- deep charcoal and near-black surfaces,
- oak and walnut browns,
- restrained bronze / aged-copper accents,
- elegant high-contrast serif typography,
- wide letter spacing in the wordmark and small labels,
- close material detail, wood grain, workshop light and tactile surfaces,
- a balance of heritage craft with contemporary architecture.

The website should feel like a high-end workshop / interior brand first. Avitus Materia OS and AI capabilities are an invisible advantage, not the dominant visual language.

## Working palette
- Ivory: `#F4F0E7`
- Warm paper: `#EBE3D6`
- Deep charcoal: `#1D1916`
- Secondary charcoal: `#28211C`
- Bronze: `#9C6C45`
- Light bronze: `#C09261`
- Oak: `#7F5B3E`
- Muted text: `#746B61`

Bronze should be used as an accent, not as the main page fill.

## Typography
### Display / brand
Use an elegant classic serif with restrained contrast. Until the final licensed/self-hosted brand font is selected, use a robust system serif stack based on Baskerville / Iowan Old Style / Times.

### Interface / body
Use a neutral modern sans-serif. Small labels may use uppercase and wider tracking.

### Rules
- headlines: serif, large, calm, low-to-medium line height,
- wordmark: uppercase serif with generous tracking,
- utility labels: small uppercase sans-serif,
- body copy: readable sans-serif with generous line height,
- avoid rounded SaaS-style typography as the dominant expression.

## Shape language
- prefer straight edges and subtle framing,
- avoid excessive pills and soft rounded cards,
- use fine rules, borders and large quiet spacing,
- forms can remain modern but should feel architectural rather than app-like,
- visual hierarchy should come from proportion, typography, contrast and material texture.

## Photography / imagery
Target photography should include:
- finished furniture in real interiors,
- macro details of old oak / natural oak,
- hands and workshop process,
- joinery and finishing details,
- reclaimed material before and after transformation,
- warm directional light and natural shadows.

Do not use generic stock-looking luxury interiors as a substitute for real Avitus Materia work once the project library is available.

## Logo direction
The approved visual references explore two useful families:
1. tree / heritage / longship symbolism,
2. a cleaner `A` monogram combined with oak / wood-grain symbolism.

The website should support either final mark. Until the trademark-ready vector logo is selected, the implementation should use a restrained temporary monogram / wordmark rather than lock the product into an unapproved graphic asset.

## Brand language
Key ideas:
- natural material,
- history of wood,
- craft,
- individual project,
- objects built for long use,
- continuity between old material and new function,
- customer participation in the project,
- modern process behind traditional craftsmanship.

Working English line: **Built for generations.**

Useful Polish territory:
- `Drewno z historią. Formy na pokolenia.`
- `Naturalny materiał nie potrzebuje przebrania.`
- `Piękno zaczyna się przed pierwszym cięciem.`

## Legacy content migration
The previous Stolarnia Drakkar website is a source of factual business history and product knowledge, but not a template to copy blindly.

Content to preserve/adapt:
- custom furniture and individual projects,
- reclaimed wood and old oak expertise,
- SORA / Rustic / Old Oak collection knowledge,
- natural oils and oil-waxes,
- customer participation in design decisions,
- workshop contact information during transition,
- emphasis on craftsmanship, durability and material quality.

Do not present `Drakkar` as the new public brand. It remains legacy/history only.

## Digital product rule
Avitus Materia OS, configurator and AI should visually inherit this same brand system. The technology layer must not turn the customer experience into a generic blue/purple SaaS interface.
